# Tester Invite Templates — Tideline v0.9.0 (DE)

Two pre-written messages for recruiting internal TestFlight testers.
**Template A** for close personal contacts (friends, fellow students).
**Template B** for ASC-team people you don't know as well.

Send these BEFORE the formal TestFlight email arrives — that email
from Apple is short and impersonal; your personal message is what
actually convinces someone to install.

Both templates assume Signal / WhatsApp / iMessage. Adapt for email by
adding a salutation and signing off.

---

## How the actual TestFlight invite arrives

After they reply with their Apple-Account email:

1. You add them to your ASC team (Users and Access → "+", choose role
   "Developer").
2. You add them to your TestFlight internal-testing group.
3. They receive an automated email from Apple with the subject *"You're
   invited to test Tideline"* and a redemption code.
4. They tap the link in the email → it opens the TestFlight app → they
   tap "Accept" → "Install".

The Apple email is impersonal. Your message above is what does the
emotional work.

## How many testers?

For v0.9.0 internal-only: 5–10 is the right size. Enough signal,
small enough that you can personally chase up on no-feedback. More
than 15 and you'll lose track of individual replies.

External testers (Beta App Review required, 1–2 day Apple wait) are
deferred to v0.9.1 per the session 10 plan.

## What if someone declines?

That's normal. Beta testing is a real ask. Thank them, move on.

## What if someone doesn't reply?

Single nudge after 3–4 days. If still no reply: drop it. Don't chase.

## Privacy norm

You're asking them to install an app that handles menstrual cycle data.
A few will find that uncomfortable to admit using. Templates above
keep it neutral — "Zyklus-Tracker" not "period app" — and don't probe
their personal cycle history. Let them volunteer that or not.
