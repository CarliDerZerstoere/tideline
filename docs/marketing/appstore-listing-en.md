## Description (max 4000 chars)

```
Tideline is a cycle tracker that runs entirely on your iPhone. No account, no cloud sync, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The app shows you what it knows and is upfront about what it doesn't.

WHAT TIDELINE DOES DIFFERENTLY

• Privacy by design: data never leaves your device. Tideline has no server, so there's nothing to leak.
• Honest uncertainty: predictions come as ranges, not false precision. "In 12–16 days" rather than "Wednesday at 6:42pm".
• No streaks, no shaming: absence isn't weakness — illness, loss, recovery are part of life. Engagement comes from usefulness, not punishment.
• No medical advice: Tideline is a wellness/lifestyle app and makes no diagnostic claims.
• Generously free: every feature works without paying. If you'd like to back development, an optional one-time contribution is available — it doesn't unlock anything.

FEATURES

• Period calendar with phase visualisation
• My Cycle overview with pattern recognition across multiple cycles
• Doctor's appointment report: PDF generated on your device, no cloud processing
• Apple Health sync, both directions (you decide)
• Optional symptom, mood, and note logging
• Events: pregnancy loss, breastfeeding, hormonal contraception, illness, travel — the app responds sensibly
• Prediction interval with honest confidence statements; the app says when it doesn't have enough data
• App lock with Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode
• German and English fully translated
• Dynamic Type support for larger text sizes

WHAT TIDELINE DOES NOT DO

• No contraceptive efficacy claims — the app is not a medical device
• No conception efficacy claims either
• No diagnostic suggestions ("you might have PCOS")
• No advertising, ever
• No tracking, no analytics, no data sale

REGULATORY STATUS

Tideline is a wellness and lifestyle app, not a medical device under EU Regulation 2017/745 (Article 2(1)). For medical questions, please consult your doctor.

ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

Tideline is built by one person in the EU. No venture capital, no advertising business model. The architecture is the privacy policy: no server means no data breach.

Feedback and bug reports via email (see support page). Typical response time 2–5 working days.

Requires iOS 18.0 or later.
```
